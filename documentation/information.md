# Perfect Charity System: project information

Perfect Charity System is an Elite Cybersecurity Specialist initiative. Its central mechanism is a direct external payment followed by a recorded internal PCS transfer, onward donation and the next fresh purchase.

## Core distinctions

- Existing banks and payment providers process money directly to the chosen receiver; PCS does not operate a fiat payment gateway.
- Internal PCS stays within the charity workflow and is not externally tradable.
- The valuation reference is 1 PCS = 1 USDT. Credit follows confirmed net payment value.
- The standard monthly allocation is 10,000 PCS. Requests for more require a reason, supporting evidence and recorded approval.
- Receiver assistance, internal operating support and external project funding have separate purposes and records.
- Directors cannot profit from any donation, directly or indirectly.
- Donors can choose public or private profiles. Receiver profiles are always public, and their contribution histories show donors.
- PCS-related earnings must be publicly disclosed. Profile privacy does not override this.
- Each country can have one inspector account, with its actions recorded.
- An internal transfer, monthly allocation and external cash receipt are different accounting events.
